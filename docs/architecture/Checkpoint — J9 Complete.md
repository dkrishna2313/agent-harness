# Harness Engineering Checkpoint — J9 Complete

**Checkpoint:** J9.3 – Strategic Engagement Platform Complete

**Date:** 1 July 2026

---

# Executive Summary

J9 completes the evolution from a strategic research platform into a Strategic Engagement Platform.

The platform no longer begins with a research question.

It now begins with an executive engagement.

That engagement is transformed into a structured Decision Architecture that drives the remainder of the reasoning pipeline.

Research has become a supporting capability rather than the primary product.
