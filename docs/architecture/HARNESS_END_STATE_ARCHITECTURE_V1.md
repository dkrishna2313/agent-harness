# 1. Vision

Harness Engineering is an **Executive Decision Intelligence Platform**.

Its purpose is to help strategy professionals transform complex business situations into evidence-backed executive decisions through structured reasoning.

The platform is designed to amplify the capabilities of experienced consultants—not replace them.

The primary user is a **Deloitte Senior Manager** leading strategic engagements and synthesizing work across multiple domains into executive recommendations.

The platform should become the foundation upon which strategy engagements are performed, documented, challenged, and evolved.

---

# 2. Product Definition

Harness Engineering is **not**:

- a chatbot
- a search engine
- a document summarizer
- a RAG application
- a presentation generator
- a PMO tool

Those are capabilities.

Harness Engineering **is**:

> **An Executive Decision Intelligence Platform that transforms strategic engagements into evidence-backed executive recommendations.**

Everything within the platform exists to improve executive decision quality.

---

# 3. Product Philosophy

The platform is built around several beliefs.

## Executive decisions are made under uncertainty.

Perfect information never exists.

The objective is not certainty.

The objective is reducing uncertainty enough to make better decisions.

---

## Evidence reduces uncertainty.

Evidence should never exist independently.

Evidence is always collected in support of a decision.

---

## Strategy is about alternatives.

The platform should never generate only one recommendation.

It should generate competing strategic options.

Each option should be evaluated objectively.

---

## Uncertainty is a first-class output.

"I don't know."

"We need more evidence."

"This assumption dominates the outcome."

These are valuable outputs.

The platform should surface them rather than hide them.

---

## Every recommendation must be explainable.

Recommendations should always answer:

- Why this?
- Why not the alternatives?
- What evidence supports it?
- What assumptions dominate it?

---

## Human judgement remains central.

The platform augments consulting teams.

Final decisions remain with humans.

---

# 4. Architectural Principles

## Principle 1

The platform reasons over **Decision Domains**, not research questions.

---

## Principle 2

Research exists only to satisfy Evidence Requirements generated by Decision Domains.

---

## Principle 3

Every recommendation must be traceable back to evidence.

---

## Principle 4

Unknowns and assumptions are first-class artifacts.

---

## Principle 5

Executive reasoning precedes research.

Research should not define the engagement.

The engagement defines the research.

---

## Principle 6

The platform should always generate multiple strategic options before converging.

---

## Principle 7

Confidence is an output.

Never an assumption.

---

## Principle 8

Every reasoning step should be inspectable.

No black boxes.

# 6. Platform Layers

## Engagement Layer

Responsible for understanding the client situation.

Inputs:

- Engagement Brief
- Objectives
- Constraints
- Stakeholders
- Success Criteria
- Risks
- Assumptions

Outputs:

- Executive Framing

---

## Decision Layer

Responsible for structuring the engagement.

Outputs:

- Decision Architecture
- Decision Domains
- Evidence Requirements

---

## Evidence Layer

Responsible for satisfying evidence requirements.

Evidence Providers may include:

- Local Knowledge Store
- Web
- SEC
- Capital IQ
- PitchBook
- SharePoint
- Internal documents
- Email
- Structured databases

The Evidence Layer does not perform strategic reasoning.

It provides trusted evidence.

---

## Reasoning Layer

Responsible for transforming evidence into strategic insight.

Activities include:

- Analysis
- Hypothesis generation
- Challenge
- Tradeoff analysis
- Option generation
- Recommendation generation
- Confidence assessment

---

## Communication Layer

Responsible for presenting reasoning.

Outputs may include:

- Executive Report
- Board Deck
- Technical Appendix
- Roadmap
- Risk Register
- Scenario Analysis

Communication should never alter reasoning.

It only changes presentation.

---

# 7. Decision Domain

The Decision Domain is the fundamental unit of reasoning.

A Decision Domain represents one executive decision that must be made.

Examples include:

- Power Strategy
- Cooling Strategy
- Capital Strategy
- Acquisition Strategy
- Operating Model
- Pricing Strategy
- Market Entry

Decision Domains are independent of industry.

Every engagement is decomposed into Decision Domains.

---

# 8. Decision Domain Lifecycle

Each Decision Domain progresses through a consistent reasoning lifecycle.

```
Decision Domain

↓

Evidence Requirements

↓

Evidence

↓

Strategic Analysis

↓

Competing Options

↓

Tradeoffs

↓

Recommendation

↓

Confidence

↓

Remaining Unknowns
```

Research Questions are generated only where additional evidence is required.

Research is therefore a supporting activity rather than the organizing principle.

---

# 9. Agent Responsibilities

The current Functional Agent pipeline is an implementation of the architecture.

Future refactoring should preserve responsibilities rather than individual agents.

Core responsibilities are:

## Executive Framing

Transform engagement into Decision Architecture.

---

## Evidence Acquisition

Collect evidence required by Decision Domains.

---

## Strategic Analysis

Interpret evidence.

Generate competing hypotheses.

Challenge reasoning.

---

## Decision Development

Generate strategic options.

Evaluate tradeoffs.

Produce recommendations.

---

## Executive Communication

Generate executive deliverables.

---

# 10. Knowledge Strategy

Knowledge should be treated as interchangeable evidence providers.

The platform should never distinguish between:

- local documents
- internet
- financial databases
- internal repositories

All become Evidence Providers.

Decision Domains request evidence.

The Knowledge Layer determines where to retrieve it.

---

# 11. Deliverables

The reasoning engine is independent of presentation.

Possible outputs include:

- Executive Strategic Report
- Board Presentation
- Technical Appendix
- Roadmap
- Risk Register
- Scenario Analysis
- Decision Log

Future deliverables should reuse the same reasoning.

# 13. Design Boundaries

The platform intentionally stops at:

- Recommendations
- Roadmaps

It does not currently extend into:

- PMO
- Delivery Management
- Project Governance

Those capabilities may exist in the future but are outside the scope of the Executive Decision Intelligence Platform.

---

# 14. Architectural North Star

Every future engineering decision should be evaluated against one question:

> **Does this move the platform closer to the End-State Architecture?**

If yes, it belongs.

If not, it should be reconsidered.

The objective is not to build more software.

The objective is to build a coherent Executive Decision Intelligence Platform.
