# AI Data Center Power — Sample Source (harness fixture)

AI data centers require significant electrical power, with GB300 NVL72 racks
drawing well over 100 kW per rack. Grid interconnection queues in preferred metro
regions can exceed 3-7 years, which pushes operators toward on-site generation and
power purchase agreements.

Liquid cooling (direct-to-chip) is generally required above roughly 50 kW per
rack; air cooling becomes impractical at GB300 NVL72 densities. Cooling
architecture therefore drives both capital cost and siting decisions.

Small modular reactors (SMRs) are being evaluated as a long-lead clean-power
option, but regulatory certification timelines of 5-10 years often fall outside a
24-month deployment window.
